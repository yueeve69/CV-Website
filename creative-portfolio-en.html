Aesop UK Digital Marketing CampaignCoursework

2026 · Marketing & Influencer

Built a full digital marketing campaign for Aesop UK — starting from PESTLE / SWOT audience and situational analysis, then landing it as an integrated "social + influencer + email" plan, consistent with the brand's premium, non-promotional tone.

Campaign objective & audience　Target UK women aged 25–34 earning ~£30–40k who value quality and social responsibility; focus on building awareness and the "consideration" stage rather than short-term sales, guiding more informed, confident purchase decisions.
Campaign Plan · Three marketing tools
1
Social Media · Instagram

Three minimalist image posts in May: ① educate on skin types and drive free in-store consultation bookings; ② recommend products by seasonal skin needs; ③ collect UGC via #AesopSkinCheck, with 20% off facials for bookings. Instagram chosen: image-led, and 33.3% of UK users are aged 25–34.

2
Influencer

Selected lifestyle / self-care creators produce soft, experience-based content — integrating Aesop into daily skincare routines and documenting in-store consultation & facial experiences, linking the online campaign to physical stores; no hard selling, no before/after.

3
Email

A confirmation email after booking reduces uncertainty; after the consultation, AI / CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot) send personalised follow-ups — routine and skincare tips based on the consultation — to strengthen conversion, fully GDPR-compliant.

HighlightFrom audience analysis (PESTLE / SWOT) to landing three concrete tools, this maps out a complete, value-led and non-promotional integrated campaign; the tutor specifically praised the "turning research into commercial recommendations" — the core skill of a marketing-strategy role.

Aesop UK · Influencer Marketing

Selected Creators & Selection Logic

Influencer selection & rationale

Rather than chasing beauty influencers alone, I selected 3 "lifestyle × skincare" creators around brand tone and target audience, using soft, experience-based content to place Aesop naturally.

Selected creators · Examples (Table 1)
Linnea Levoskin
@linnealevoskin
87.6k followers

A lifestyle & skincare creator focused on minimal routines, natural aesthetics and calm, mindful self-care.

Akhalia Fearon
@akhalia.fearon
69.9k followers

A wellness & lifestyle creator sharing daily routines and hair; emphasises authenticity and a balanced approach to beauty.

Yezi.Beauty
@yezi.beauty
351k followers

A skincare-focused creator producing product reviews, routines and knowledge, centred on ingredient awareness and everyday skin concerns.

Selection logic

  • Fit the brand tone: minimalist style and calm voice, echoing sustainability and mindful consumption
  • Beyond beauty: include lifestyle / wellness creators to place Aesop in a wider lifestyle context
  • Age relevance: all fall within the 25–34 core audience for stronger relatability
  • Diverse backgrounds: creators of diverse ethnicities reinforce a "for all skin types and people" image

Collaboration & goals

  • Soft, experience-based content that integrates the product into daily life, not direct promotion
  • Document in-store consultation / facial experiences, linking online campaign with physical stores
  • Clearly disclose sponsored content for transparency and authenticity
  • Aim to build visibility and long-term relationships rather than immediate sales
Why this selectionCreator selection is where marketing-strategy judgement shows: weighing brand positioning, target audience and content ethics together, not just follower count. The full plan and creator list are included in the portfolio PDF.
